On the name

Solkast is Swedish for the way light gets thrown.

Not the sun itself — the cast of it. The long shadow at four in the afternoon, the shape a thing makes when the light hits it sideways. That felt like the right idea for clothes meant to be worn for years rather than noticed for a season.

Everything is printed to order in the EU. Nothing is held in a warehouse, nothing is made in a quantity we hope to shift. You order a piece; it gets made; it comes to you. It takes a few days longer and wastes considerably less.

How it is made

Four things we hold to.

Organic cotton

Ring-spun, mid to heavy weight. GOTS-certified fibre, chosen so a piece holds its shape past the first year.

Printed to order

Made after you order it, in the EU. No deadstock, no end-of-season pallet going somewhere it shouldn't.

Unisex cuts

Relaxed and oversized fits drafted to sit the same on most bodies. Sizes S through 2XL.

Thirty days

Unworn and unwashed, returned inside thirty days, refunded in full. Longer than the law asks.
